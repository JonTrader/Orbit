# Daily and Monthlies are separate domains

Orbit treats day-to-day to-dos and recurring monthly obligations as different kinds of commitment, not filters on one list. Daily holds Tasks; Monthlies holds Monthlies. You cannot reclassify one into the other by moving Sections - wrong kind means delete and recreate. Custom `tasks` / `notes` / `mixed` Sections cover other organization needs without collapsing that split.

**Considered options:** One unified Task model with section-gated behavior; one merged list with recurrence and views. Rejected because they blur reminders, Upcoming, and the household mental model the product is built around.
